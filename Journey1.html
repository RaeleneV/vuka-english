Journey 01

Is Your Business Ready to Grow?

"You're thinking about a second stall, more stock, or your first helper. But how do you actually know if you're ready, or just hopeful?" Growing too soon, before your foundation is strong, can put a trader into debt or under strain. This journey helps you check your own signs before you spend a single extra rand.

The Five Signs You're Actually Ready

"Every trader dreams of expanding. But how many actually check if the timing is right first?"

Business advisors point to a consistent set of signs that show a business has a strong enough base to grow safely. Your income is steady or growing over several months, not just on the odd good week. A high number of your customers come back again and again, which shows real trust. You regularly sell out or have to turn customers away, meaning there's more demand than you can currently meet. Your notebook records are in order, so you can actually see your profit trend over time. And you have some savings or a buffer, so you're not relying on every rand of today's profit to restock tomorrow.

💡 QUICK TIP Don't wait until all five signs are perfect. Even having three or four in place is often enough to take one small, careful step.

What "Growing" Actually Looks Like

"Growing doesn't always mean a whole new shop. What's the smallest next step that would actually take you further?"

Growth doesn't have to mean a second stall straight away. For a food trader, it can look like adding a new product line, extending your trading hours for flexibility, taking on a helper, or, eventually, opening a second spot. The smaller the step, the smaller the risk if it doesn't work out exactly as planned.

💡 QUICK TIP Match your growth step to your strongest sign. If you keep selling out, extending your hours or adding stock is a safer first move than renting a second stall.
