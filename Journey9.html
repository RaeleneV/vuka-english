Journey 09

Ready for Funding?

"You assumed you couldn't apply for funding because you're not registered with CIPC. Is that actually true?" Many informal traders assume funding isn't for them. In reality, several South African funders specifically support informal and unregistered micro-enterprises, including street traders and vendors.

Funding That Doesn't Need CIPC

"You assumed you couldn't apply for funding because you're not registered. Is that actually true?"

The NYDA Grant Programme, run by the National Youth Development Agency, specifically supports businesses operating informally or formally, including street traders and vendors, as long as the business has a profit motive and is commercially capable of success. It's available to youth business owners aged 18 to 35, and you can apply through any NYDA branch, online, or by calling 0800 58 58 58. The SEDFA Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme, formed from the merger of SEFA, SEDA, and the CBDA, specifically targets township and rural businesses and supports formalising informal ones. Stokvels and savings groups aren't a "formal" funder, but they're a trusted, community-based way for many South African traders to access lump sums for stock or equipment without high interest.

💡 QUICK TIP Funders that support informal traders often get fewer applications than you'd expect. Not being registered yet shouldn't stop you from asking.

Get Funding-Ready Before You Need It

"A funding opportunity opens tomorrow with a two-week deadline. Could you apply in time?"

Start preparing now, before you actually apply. You'll typically need your ID, proof of your trading history (this is exactly why your notebook and bank account matter, they're the proof you can show a funder), a simple description of what the funding will be used for ("a second refrigerator to reduce spoilage," or "a bulk stock discount for the festive season"), and, if it's a loan rather than a grant, a basic idea of how you'll repay it.

💡 QUICK TIP Prepare your documents in one folder, physical or on your phone, before you need them, not while a deadline is ticking.
