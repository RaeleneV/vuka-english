About Vuka

Closing the information gap for food-sector entrepreneurs.

Many small food business owners want to formalise and grow, but the information they need is scattered, hard to find, or written for a different audience entirely. Vuka brings it into one free, structured place.

  • Written specifically for home-based, container-based, and mobile food sellers.
  • No login, no cost, and a downloadable PDF version for offline or low-data access.
  • Vuka is an offline platform. However, your first interaction with the website should take place while you are connected to the internet.
  • Once you have fully interacted with the website you may disconnect from the internet and you will still have full access to the website's content and functionality including access to the PDFs.
  • Built with a Calculate Your Capital tool so you can budget before you commit money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Vuka?

No. Vuka is completely free to use and you don't need to sign up, log in, or create an account. Everything is available straight away.

Do I need to pay for the content?

No, all Journeys, topics, and the Calculate Your Capital tool are free to use, with no hidden costs.

How can I download the material?

Every journey has a Download PDF option where you can download the respective journey information, and you can also download all journeys at once from the Download PDF Version page in the footer. This is useful if you have limited or costly internet access.
